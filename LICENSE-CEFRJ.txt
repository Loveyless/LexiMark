LexiMark uses CEFR-J Wordlist as the data source for A1/A2/B1/B2 leveled
word lists. The transformed JSON files under `public/data/levels/` are
re-distributions of CEFR-J data, and therefore inherit the same CC BY-SA 4.0
license. Any downstream use must:

  1. Provide attribution to CEFR-J.
  2. Distribute under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

For the canonical wordlist and additional usage notes, see the CEFR-J site.
